Kayou Winx Club Fairy Diary Card Tracker & Checklist

A free fan-made checklist for the Kayou Winx Club: Fairy Diary collectible cards, Series 1. Tick off every card you own, browse them by rarity, filter by what you still need, already own or have spares of, and note serial numbers. Everything saves in your browser — no signup, no account.

Editions

  • Magic Edition — 123 cards across 14 rarities. 8 cards per pack, 12 packs per box. Releasing November 2026.
  • Daily Edition — 94 cards across 9 rarities. 5 cards per pack, 20 packs per box. Releasing November 2026.

62 cards (all UR, TR, SSR, SR and R) are the same in both editions, so owning one counts in both.

Rarities & tiers

Rarest first: SE (Scenario-E), BP (Brush Rare), CR (Creative Rare), ZR (Zenith Rare), AR (Artist Rare), UGR (Ultra Golden Rare), UR (Ultra Rare), TR (Titan Rare), HR (Holo Rare), SSR (Super Spark Rare), FR (Function Rare), ◇SR (Super Rare parallel), SR (Super Rare), QR (Cute Rare) and R (Rare).

Characters

The Winx — Bloom, Stella, Flora, Musa, Tecna and Aisha — plus the Specialists (Sky, Brandon, Helia, Riven, Timmy, Nabu), the Trix (Icy, Darcy, Stormy) and the Winx pets (Milly, Belle, Coco, Pepe, Ginger, Chicko).
